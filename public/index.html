Get to Know Us

CV. Adi Makmur Permata founded in 2024, located in Purwokerto Central Java Indonesia is a company engaged in processing coconut fiber. We produce processed coconut fiber product as Cocobristle, Cocopeat, and Cocofiber under the Cocoadi Brand. Our processed products are superior products that use quality coconut fiber raw materials and have export specifications and international standardization. Supported by professional employee, we are commited to prioritizing customer satisfactionand maintaining product quality.

What We Provide

1.COCOBRISTLE

Cocobristle is the longest and strongest variety of coir fiber. It is made from naturally retted busks using a defibering machine. This fiber is used for the finest and most high end application of coir fiber due to its length strength and the ease of combing them into uniform ties.

Details:

  • Material: Coconut Coir Bristle
  • Color: Gold Brown
  • Pattern: Raw
  • Length: 25-28 cm
  • Moisture: 17%
Produced by CV. Adi Makmur Permata. Purwokerto, Central Java, Indonesia

2.COCOFIBER

Is coconut fiber which is shaped like long hair with a smooth and strong texture. It is used as a raw material for handicrafts, car seats, mattresses, ropes, and livestock pens.

Details:

  • Material: Coconut Coir Bristle
  • Color: Gold Brown
  • Pattern: Raw
  • Length: 25-28 cm
  • Moisture: 17%
Produced by CV. Adi Makmur Permata. Purwokerto, Central Java, Indonesia

3.COCOPEAT

Is an organic planting medium that replaces soil, made from coconut fiber which is processed into fine granules.Has many benefits,including pest resistance, high water absorption and enviromentally friendliness.

Details:

  • Material: Coconut Coir Bristle
  • Color: Gold Brown
  • Pattern: Raw
  • Length: 25-28 cm
  • Moisture: 17%
Produced by CV. Adi Makmur Permata. Purwokerto, Central Java, Indonesia
